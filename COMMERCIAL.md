## What the AGPL does and does not do

Stated plainly, because this is widely misunderstood:

- **It does not prohibit commercial use.** Open-source licences cannot; the
  Open Source Definition requires permitting any field of endeavour.
- **It does not require you to publish anything if you only run it
  internally.** A company may run this pipeline on its own hardware, design
  binders, and owe nothing. Copyleft attaches to *distribution*, and AGPL
  section 13 additionally to *network service* - not to use.
- **It does require** that anyone who distributes a modified version, or
  offers one as a network service, releases their modifications under the same
  terms.

If you need an obligation the AGPL does not create, that is what the commercial
licence is for.
